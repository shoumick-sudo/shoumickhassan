Data quality and publication rules

  • Immutable raw layer and report-version audit trail.
  • Reject the entire submission version when a critical row defect appears.
  • Choose the latest approved version, even when a newer draft exists.
  • Keep received, approved and on-time completeness separate.
  • Keep sector outputs separate. Do not add different event types into a beneficiary total.
Definitions and limitations

Approval completeness = approved reports / expected reports. Receipt completeness = received reports / expected reports. Timeliness = reports first received by the due date / expected reports. A receipt can be on time yet contain invalid data. Targets are synthetic planned event volumes at report grain. Approval dates, authentication and real multi-user permissions are outside this local demonstrator.

Facility snapshot

Latest observations: 30 September 2026. Cutoff: 16 October 2026. All observations are 16 days old and exceed a seven-day freshness rule. Treat them as historical snapshots, not current emergency status. Unknown status is reported separately.
